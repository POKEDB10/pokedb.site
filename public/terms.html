1. Service Overview

pokedb.site provides developer utilities including a URL shortener (TinyURL), encrypted text storage (Paste), temporary direct file transfer (Drop), and offline developer tools. All services are offered as-is for personal and non-commercial utility.

2. Acceptable Use & Prohibited Content

You agree not to use pokedb.site services for any unlawful purpose. Specifically, you may not submit, store, or transmit:

  • Content that violates applicable local or international laws;
  • Malware, viruses, ransomware, trojans, phishing lures, or credential harvesting targets;
  • Unsolicited commercial bulk spam or automated scraping traffic;
  • Content that infringes copyright, trademark, trade secrets, or intellectual property rights.

Executable file formats (.exe, .msi, .dll, .apk) uploaded to Drop are blocked by default unless client-side encrypted.

3. Notice and Takedown Procedure (Abuse Contact Notice)

pokedb.site does not maintain a registered designated agent with the United States Copyright Office and does not assert safe-harbor immunity under 17 U.S.C. § 512. However, we respect intellectual property rights and promptly investigate all valid complaints.

To report copyright infringement, trademark abuse, malware, or unlawful content, please submit an abuse notification via our Abuse Contact Form or email abuse@pokedb.site with:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work or right claimed to have been infringed;
  • The specific URL, short code, or drop link on pokedb.site hosting the contested material;
  • Sufficient contact information to permit follow-up communication;
  • A good-faith statement that the use is unauthorized.

Content confirmed to be malicious or infringing will be expeditiously disabled, quarantined, or removed.

4. Automated Abuse Mitigation & Quarantine

pokedb.site employs automated creation-time threat intelligence and crowd-sourced abuse reporting. If content receives 3 independent abuse reports from distinct network subnets, it is automatically quarantined (HTTP 451) pending manual administrative takedown or review.
